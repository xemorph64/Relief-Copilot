Civic tech · Maharashtra disaster relief

Relief Copilot

A WhatsApp-native assistant that turns a spoken damage report — a voice note in Marathi or Hindi, photos and a GPS pin — into a rule-matched, evidence-backed, submission-ready government compensation claim.

How it works

  1. Voice note, not paperworkThe affected citizen describes what happened in their own language. No app to install, no forms to fill.
  2. Speech → structured claimBhashini open-source speech models transcribe; an LLM extracts dates, hazard, assets and location — never money or eligibility.
  3. Rules, not vibesA deterministic engine matches the claim against official Maharashtra GRs (fingerprint-verified PDFs, two-person sign-off) to produce the eligible amount with citations.
  4. Submission-ready PDFThe claim is delivered back as a bilingual PDF with evidence attached — ready to file with the talathi.

Design principles

Phone number = identity

No app installs, no logins. The channel citizens already trust.

Evidence-native

Every rupee traces to a published government resolution with page and quote references.

Rules are signed

No rule affects an amount until two reviewers sign it against the GR PDF.

Retroactive redress

New GRs re-open old claims; citizens are told their compensation may increase.

Built with

WhatsApp Business Platform

Citizen-facing channel with signed webhooks and idempotent intake.

Bhashini (ULCA)

Government speech stack — Marathi/Hindi ASR and TTS.

Deterministic rule engine

Python + SHA-256 signed GR PDFs; LGD district masters via data.gov.in.

Caseworker dashboard

Confidence-sorted review queue for human officials, live event stream.
